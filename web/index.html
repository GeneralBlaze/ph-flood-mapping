      Limitations
      • It maps standing water seen by satellite radar (Sentinel-1), which sees through cloud. Street flooding that drains within hours can be missed.
      • Radar struggles between tall buildings and under dense trees, so dense built-up areas are likely under-counted.
      • Areas are neighbourhood-level. The map cannot point to an individual drain or culvert.
      • A flooded area may have blocked drainage or may simply be built on natural floodplain. The map alone cannot tell these apart.
      • Every result is a lead to check on the ground, not a conclusion.
